## English (for your reference — do not send)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to request an archival search and the issuance of **certified copies** of archival
documents that I need in support of an application for citizenship of the Slovak Republic by
descent (under § 7(2)(j) of Act No. 40/1993 Coll. on citizenship of the Slovak Republic).

My great-grandfather was known in the United States as **Sam Miller**, originally surnamed

I would kindly ask you to search for and provide copies of the following documents concerning the

1. the **domovský list** / certificate of **right of domicile** (domicile pertinency) of the

2. the **1921 population census sheets** (and, if available, 1930) for the household;
3. the **birth record / birth certificate of the son born on 31 December 1904** (the entry may
   have been made in early 1905);
4. any other documents evidencing the family's **right of domicile or Czechoslovak citizenship**.

I would also be grateful for information on whether these documents are held in your collections,
the fees for the search and for the copies, how payment from abroad can be made, and whether
**certified (officially authenticated) copies** usable for official purposes can be issued.

### Notes
- **Why these four items:** Sam emigrated in 1922, so he is not in the 1930 census. The
  **domovský list** and the **1921 census** are the documents that actually establish his
  Czechoslovak citizenship (via *domovská príslušnosť* / right of domicile). Item 3 also asks for
  the birth record in case their copy is cleaner/officially issuable.
- **Name forms given deliberately:** both *Meller* (original) and *Miller* (US), *Meller Pál* /
  *Pavol Meller* for the father, and the 1904↔1905 date note, so the archivist can match the
  record despite the variations.
- If you'd rather they reply in English, add: *"V prípade potreby môžete odpovedať aj v anglickom
  jazyku."* ("If needed, you may also reply in English.")
